## What we do for cats

### TNVR programs

Trap, neuter, vaccinate and release (TNVR) programs are organized to improve the lives of stray cats and limit the birth of new kittens on the streets.


### Rescuing and fostering

Cats in need of further veterinary care are cared for and placed with local foster families, partner veterinary clinics or shelters for their recovery.


### Adoptions

Each adoption, national or international, not only gives a second chance to a cat waiting for a home but also frees up a place in a shelter or foster home for those in need.


### Support shelters and rescuers

Individual rescuers and shelters can receive help in donation, equipment donation, adoption support or documentation such as adoption contracts, foster contracts etc.


### Education & information

A compassionate contact with each adopter is one of our priority, in events or online, informing more and more people about the situation of street cats in Greece.
